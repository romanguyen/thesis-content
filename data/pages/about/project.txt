====== Project MetaCentrum ======
Short description of activities, mission, and history of the MetaCentrum.

MetaCentrum is a virtual organization that operates and manages distributed computing infrastructure in the Czech Republic. It provides computational resources to the academic community and research institutions.

===== Mission =====
The main mission is to provide high-performance computing resources and support for scientific research across various disciplines.

===== History =====
MetaCentrum was established as part of CESNET's activities to support the Czech research community with advanced computing infrastructure.
