====== Perun ======

Perun system is used for managing users' identities, groups and access to resources and services in distributed enviroments. Perun has been integrated with a number of infrastructures and projects on both national and international level and CESNET cooperates on its design and development. More information can be found on [[https://perun.cesnet.cz/web/]].
