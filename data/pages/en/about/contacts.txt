====== Contacts ======

**Imported contact directory:** addresses, travel instructions and maps are archival; confirm them with [[mailto:meta@cesnet.cz|MetaCentrum support]] before visiting. The maps are not live navigation.

If for some reason you need to personally meet one of our user support workers, you can do so at following addresses:
