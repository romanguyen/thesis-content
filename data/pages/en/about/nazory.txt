**RNDr. Václav Martínek Ph.D., RNDr. Petr Jeřábek Ph.D., Univerzita Karlova**

Thank you for the opportunity to use MetaCentrum resources, for the continuous improvement of HW and SW, and for all the help with solving occasional problems. This year we used the pilot operation of parallel computations with CUDA acceleration on the konos1-10 machines. Thanks to the combination of 12 CPUs and 2 CUDA-enabled graphics cards, some types of MD computations became very significantly (5 to 6 times) faster. Given the growing number of applications supporting CUDA and the expected significant performance increase of the new generation of graphics cards with the Kepler core (28 nm), this kind of computation acceleration seems very promising in the coming years as well.
