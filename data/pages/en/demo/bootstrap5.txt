====== Bootstrap 5 components ======

This page is written only in wiki syntax. The Bootstrap 5 components come from the ComboStrap extension; the page shell, navigation and styling come from the project template.

<jumbotron>
===== Hero block =====
A lead paragraph with a call to action. Requested for the home page hero (CESNET ''index'' pattern).

<button type="primary">Primary action</button> <button type="secondary">Secondary action</button>
</jumbotron>

===== Number tiles =====

Tiles for key figures that can link to other pages (IT4I-style).

<grid>
<box class="border rounded p-3 text-center">
=== 128 ===
compute nodes
</box>
<box class="border rounded p-3 text-center">
=== 4096 ===
CPU cores
</box>
<box class="border rounded p-3 text-center">
=== 12 ===
GPU servers
</box>
</grid>

===== Cards =====

A project or hardware teaser.

<grid>

=== Project card ===

</card>

</card>
</grid>
