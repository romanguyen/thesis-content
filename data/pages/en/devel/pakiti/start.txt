====== Pakiti ======

===== Introduction =====
We are developing Pakiti service for machines detection that are not kept up-to-date with security patches. Unpatched security vulnerabilities pose a significant risk for large infrastructures and utilizing the Pakiti service allows the security staff to mitigate the risk. We are providing that service also for the whole EGI infrastructure.
