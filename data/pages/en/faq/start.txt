===== Basics =====

==== What is MetaCentrum NGI? ====

MetaCentrum is the Czech **National Grid Infrastructure (NGI)**, operated as part of CESNET's activities. It connects computing and storage resources from research and academic institutions and makes them available for scientific collaboration.

Read more on the [[en:about:start|About MetaCentrum]] page.

==== What is the difference between MetaCentrum NGI and MetaCentrum VO? ====

**NGI** refers to the national infrastructure and its coordination. A **virtual organization (VO)** is a group of users who share resources and access permissions within that infrastructure.

MetaCentrum VO provides its members with access to computing and storage capacity. See the [[en:vo:start|virtual organizations]] page for an overview of managed organizations.

==== Where can I get help with a question or problem? ====

For questions about your account, computing or MetaCentrum services, contact **[[mailto:meta@cesnet.cz|meta@cesnet.cz]]**. For broader e-INFRA CZ enquiries, use [[mailto:einfra-support@einfra.cz|einfra-support@einfra.cz]].

Include the following in your request:

  * your username,
  * the job ID and computing environment, if relevant,
  * the error message or steps needed to reproduce the problem.

See the [[https://docs.metacentrum.cz/en/docs/support|user support guide]] for further recommendations.

==== What is EGI? ====

[[https://www.egi.eu/|EGI]] connects European research infrastructures and supports shared use of computing and data resources. National infrastructures, including MetaCentrum, help research communities collaborate across institutions and countries.

===== Membership =====

==== What are the benefits of MetaCentrum membership? ====

Membership gives you access to a shared environment for research and collaboration:

  * computing and storage capacity,
  * scientific software and data-processing tools,
  * collaboration and resource sharing between research teams.

See the [[en:grid:start|grid overview]] and [[https://docs.metacentrum.cz/en/docs/welcome|service documentation]] for more information.

==== Who can use MetaCentrum and how do I get an account? ====

MetaCentrum is available to members, students and employees of Czech academic and non-commercial research institutions for research and educational purposes. Registration requires a valid academic affiliation; other applicants should contact user support first.

  - Check the [[https://docs.metacentrum.cz/en/docs/access/terms|terms and conditions]].
  - Follow the [[https://docs.metacentrum.cz/en/docs/access/account|account registration guide]] and complete the application. Applications are approved manually.
  - After approval, follow the [[https://docs.metacentrum.cz/en/docs/computing/run-basic-job|guide to running your first computing job]].

===== Virtual organizations =====

==== Do I need my own virtual organization? ====

You do not need to establish your own organization for ordinary use of resources through MetaCentrum VO. A separate VO may suit a research community that needs its own membership management and resource-sharing policies.

Discuss your requirements with [[mailto:meta@cesnet.cz|user support]].

==== How do I establish a virtual organization? ====

Contact the MetaCentrum administrators through [[mailto:meta@cesnet.cz|user support]] first. Describe the community's purpose, expected number of members and computing or storage requirements.

The administrators can discuss suitable membership management, access policies and options for joining the infrastructure.

==== How does a virtual organization get computing resources? ====

A VO receives access through agreements with resource providers and the infrastructure's access policies. Available capacity may depend on project conditions and assigned permissions.

Contact the MetaCentrum administrators to discuss additional capacity or contributing your own resources.
