====== Collaborative environments seminar ======

//Ivana Křenková, 12 November 2010//

CESNET association will held a collaborative environments seminar on Thursday,** 2 December 2010** (Prague) devoted to variety of voice and video broadcast technologies (from IP telephony to uncompressed high-definition video). Seminar will be held in Czech language.

More information are available at [[http://metavo.metacentrum.cz/cs/seminars/seminar3/index.html|http://www.cesnet.cz/akce/2010/vzdalena-spoluprace/]]
