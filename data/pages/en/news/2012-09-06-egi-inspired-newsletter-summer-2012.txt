====== EGI Inspired Newsletter - Summer 2012 ======

//Ivana Křenková, 6 September 2012//

Summer 2012 issue of our Inspired newsletter is now online!

  * html version - [[http://go.egi.eu/Summer12]]
  * PDF version - [[http://go.egi.eu/Summer12PDF]]

This issue features:

  * A preview of the Technical Forum in Prague
  * Steve Brewer summarises the EGI Federated Cloud Task Force's activities
  * The MPI Virtual Team reports on a number of problems solved
  * Stephen Burke & Jelena Tamuliene talk about their secondment projects
  * Sara Coelho profiles VO auger
  * Sergio Andreozzi ponders the costs of e-science and introduces the first numbers
  * Gergely Sipos writes about the latest developments in Federated Identity Management
  * Owen Appleton rounds up the achievements of the gSLM project

As always, your comments/feedback and ideas for stories are very
welcome – please email [[mailto:press@egi.eu|press@egi.eu]].
