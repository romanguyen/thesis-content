====== Grid computing ======

A grid connects computing and storage resources from different institutions into a shared environment. MetaCentrum makes this infrastructure available for scientific computing, data analysis and collaboration between research teams.

  * [[#how_the_grid_works|How the grid works]]
  * [[#benefits_for_users|Benefits for users]]
  * [[#getting_started|Getting started]]
  * [[#contributing_resources|Contributing resources]]

===== How the grid works =====

Computing clusters and storage capacity remain with their providers but are available through shared services and access policies. Users can work with resources across participating institutions.

MetaCentrum coordinates the Czech National Grid Infrastructure (NGI). Computing jobs are submitted to the PBS scheduler, which allocates available resources according to job requirements and infrastructure policies.

[[en:resources:hardware:start|Explore computing resources]] · [[https://docs.metacentrum.cz/en/docs/computing/run-basic-job|Computing documentation]]

===== Benefits for users =====

  * **Shared computing capacity** for simulations, analysis and batch processing.
  * **Scientific software** from a range of research fields.
  * **Data workflows** and collaboration between teams at different institutions.
  * **Job management and monitoring** through MetaCentrum tools.
  * **User support** for questions about the environment and services.

===== Getting started =====

  - **Get an account.** See the [[en:faq:start#membership|membership FAQ]] and [[https://docs.metacentrum.cz/en/docs/access/account|registration guide]].
  - **Prepare your first job.** Follow the [[https://docs.metacentrum.cz/en/docs/computing/run-basic-job|basic computing job tutorial]].
  - **Choose software and resources.** Use the [[https://docs.metacentrum.cz/en/docs/software|software documentation]] and [[en:resources:hardware:start|hardware overview]].

For browser-based access to the computing environment, see [[https://docs.metacentrum.cz/en/docs/graphical/ondemand|OnDemand]].

===== Contributing resources =====

Academic and research institutions can discuss contributing their computing or storage capacity with MetaCentrum. The shared environment supports resource sharing and collaboration between institutions.

Areas of cooperation include:

  * selecting, installing and operating computing clusters,
  * managing accounts, software environments and job scheduling,
  * operational monitoring and technical troubleshooting,
  * access policies for dedicated and shared capacity.

Contact [[mailto:meta@cesnet.cz|meta@cesnet.cz]] to discuss options. Describe your institution, available resources and intended use.

===== European collaboration =====

MetaCentrum participates in European research collaboration. [[https://www.egi.eu/|EGI]] connects infrastructures and research communities across countries. See the [[en:faq:start|FAQ]] for an explanation of NGI and virtual organizations.
