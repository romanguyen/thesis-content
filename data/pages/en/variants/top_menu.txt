====== Top Navigation Variant ======

This page collects sample links to combinations of layout and style.
The header offers the same choice under Preferences (Layout and Style).

===== Demo links =====

  * [[en:variants:news?layout=left|News – variant (overview + archive), Left menu]]
  * [[en:variants:it4i-elements?style=metacentrum|IT4I elements – variant, MetaCentrum design]]
  * [[en:news:start?layout=left&style=cesnet|News – Left menu, CESNET look]]
  * [[en:start?layout=left&style=cesnet|Home page – Left menu, CESNET look]]

===== Notes =====

  * `layout=top` / `layout=left` match the Top menu / Left menu choices.
  * `style=cesnet` / `style=metacentrum` / `style=bootstrap5` match the CESNET look / MetaCentrum design / Bootstrap 5 choices.
  * Without these parameters, Top menu and CESNET look are used.
