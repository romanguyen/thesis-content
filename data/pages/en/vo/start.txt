====== Vo ======

Users of the European Grid Infrastructure are organised into Virtual Organisations (VO). A VO is a group of people who share similar interests, have similar goals, and need to work collaboratively sharing resources (e.g. data, software, expertise, CPU, storage space) regardless of their geographical location. There are already more than [[http://operations-portal.egi.eu/vo|200 VOs hosted on EGI]].**** Users are invited to join these VOs, or establish new ones for their own communities.
