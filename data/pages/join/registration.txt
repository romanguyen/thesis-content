====== Registration ======
Complete the registration process to get access to MetaCentrum resources.

===== Registration Steps =====
  - Verify your eligibility (member of Czech academic institution)
  - Prepare your eduID.cz identity
  - Fill in the registration form in Perun
  - Wait for approval (usually within 1 working day)

[[https://perun.metacentrum.cz|Start Registration]]
