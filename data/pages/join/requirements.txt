====== Requirements ======
Requirements and eligibility criteria for MetaCentrum membership.

===== Eligibility =====
  * Member of a Czech academic or research institution
  * Valid eduID.cz identity or Hostel eduID.cz guest account
  * Research or educational purpose for using the resources

===== Acceptable Use =====
  * Resources must be used for academic/research purposes
  * Commercial use requires separate agreement
  * Users must comply with CESNET acceptable use policies
