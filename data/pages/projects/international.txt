====== International Projects ======
International projects and collaborations that MetaCentrum participates in.

  * [[https://www.egi.eu|EGI (European Grid Initiative)]] - Pan-European e-infrastructure for research computing, providing advanced computing services.
  * [[https://eosc.eu|EOSC (European Open Science Cloud)]] - European initiative for open science, FAIR data, and research services.
  * [[https://www.geant.org|GEANT]] - Pan-European research and education network connecting NRENs.
  * [[https://prace-ri.eu|PRACE]] - Partnership for Advanced Computing in Europe providing HPC resources.
