====== Data Storage ======
Storage resources and data management in MetaCentrum.

===== Storage Types =====
  * **Home directories:** Personal storage, backed up
  * **Scratch:** High-performance temporary storage
  * **Archive:** Long-term data preservation
  * **Project storage:** Shared storage for collaborations

===== Data Transfer =====
Use SCP, SFTP, or GridFTP for transferring data to and from MetaCentrum.
