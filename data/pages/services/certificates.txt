====== Certificates ======
Digital certificates for secure access to grid resources and services.

===== CESNET CA Certificates =====
Personal certificates issued by CESNET Certificate Authority for accessing grid services.

===== How to Get a Certificate =====
  - Visit the CESNET PKI portal
  - Authenticate with your eduID.cz identity
  - Request a personal certificate
  - Install the certificate in your browser and system

[[https://pki.cesnet.cz|Go to CESNET PKI]]
